How many sellers will we sign next month, and what will they sell?

Pipeline and revenue forecasting on Olist's real seller acquisition funnel: 8,000 marketing qualified leads from June 2017 to May 2018, the deals the sales team closed, and every order those sellers went on to make. Code and method · One page memo

Leads in, deals out

New qualified leads by contact month and deals won by month. The sales team closed its first deal in December 2017.

Lead score, tested on later leads

Leads from March to May 2018 split into ten equal groups by a score trained only on January and February leads. Share won within 90 days.

Monthly deals from the existing pipeline

Forecast made at the start of each month from leads already in the pipeline, against two run rate rules.

Which deals bring the revenue

Share of deals against share of the sellers' first 90 days of sales, by the lead type the sales rep recorded.

Forecasting the next cohort's sales

Each month's new sellers, forecast from sellers signed in earlier months. A plain average per deal is pulled up by a few very large sellers. Capping each past deal at the 99th percentile fixes most of it.
